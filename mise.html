Getting started

Carte is your private chef business hub — clients, bookings, menus, and records in one place, synced across all your devices.

1 Set up your profile — tap the gold ⚙️ in the top-right to add your business name, logo, and staff. These appear on booking reports and PDF exports.
2 Add your clients — tap Clients in the nav. Client details auto-fill into jobs and transport logs so you only type them once.
3 Build your dish library — tap Menus & Dishes. Add dishes once, group them into saved menus, then attach any menu to a booking.
4 Log a booking — tap Jobs and use + Book a New Job. Fill in the date, client, covers, and menu. Upcoming jobs appear on your home dashboard.
Jobs & bookings

Each job stores the date, time, client, location, covers, job type, notes, and one or more menus. Menus on jobs are frozen snapshots — editing your library later won't change past bookings.

Upcoming jobs are shown soonest-first at the top of the Jobs tab. Past bookings are grouped by month under View previous bookings.

The Calendar tab shows all bookings at a glance. Tap a day to see what's booked, tap a job to open it directly.

Your next upcoming booking also appears as a banner on the home screen — tap it to see full details.

Booking PDF reports

Tap Jobs, scroll to the bottom, and tap Export PDF to generate a branded booking report — all jobs grouped by month, with menus listed underneath each booking.

On iPhone, tap the share icon in Safari and choose Save to Files or Print. On Android, use the browser menu to save as PDF.

Kitchen records

Carte includes record types for private chef compliance:

🚗 Transport Temps — log food temperatures in transit
🍳 Kitchen Assessment — check client kitchens before service
🌾 Allergen Log — record 14-allergen information per service
📜 Credentials — track certifications with 90-day expiry alerts

These are in the More tab.

Syncing with Veriqo

Carte and Veriqo share the same account. Clients, menus, dishes, and credentials sync automatically between both apps every time you sign in.

Jobs booked in Carte also appear in Veriqo's Next Booking banner, so you can see your upcoming work in either app.

The Suite plan gives you full access to both apps under one subscription.

Account & subscription

Sign in with your email address or Google. Your data is tied to your account and syncs across every device you sign in to.

Trial: New accounts get a free trial with full access. Your trial end date is shown in Settings under Subscription.

Subscribing: Carte is £12/month or £120/year. The annual plan saves you two months. To get both Carte and Veriqo, the Suite plan is £20/month or £200/year.

Managing your subscription: Active subscribers can tap Manage subscription → in Settings to open the Stripe billing portal.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except Supabase (our cloud database provider, based in the EU).

How your data is stored

Your data is stored in two places: on your device (in your browser's local storage) and in our cloud database (Supabase, EU servers). Both copies are protected — only you can access your own records.

We use row-level security so no other user can read your data, even if they know your user ID.

Data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@getveriqo.co.uk.

Save Carte as an app

Carte works best saved to your home screen — it opens full-screen like a native app, loads faster, and works offline.

iPhone & iPad — Safari only
1 Open getveriqo.co.uk/mise.html in Safari — Chrome & Firefox on iPhone cannot install apps
2 Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the icon looks like a box with an arrow pointing up:
3 Scroll down in the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen ＋ Add to Home Screen
4 Tap Add in the top-right — Carte appears on your home screen with its own icon
Android — Chrome
1 Open Carte in Chrome
2 Tap the three-dot menu ⋮ in the top-right of Chrome
3 Tap Add to Home screen or Install app
4 Tap Install to confirm

Once installed, Carte opens full-screen with no browser bar. Your data stays on the device and syncs to the cloud when you sign in.
