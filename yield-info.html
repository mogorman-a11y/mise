Menu costing & profit analysis

Add ingredients with quantities and prices, set your labour hours and mileage, and Yield calculates your food cost percentage, effective hourly rate, and profit margin in real time. A circular gauge shows your efficiency vs your target rate at a glance.

  • Food cost % with wastage buffer
  • Labour cost & effective hourly rate
  • HMRC mileage at £0.45/mile
  • ✨ Scan Receipt — auto-import ingredients
  • Load dishes from your Carte menu library

Quotes, extras & client portal

Build a quote from your costing in one tap. Add staffing, equipment hire, travel, and gratuity as line-item extras. Send it to the client with a professional email — they get a branded portal page showing the full breakdown, deposit amount, and your payment instructions.

  • Quote builder with VAT support
  • Extras: staffing, equipment, travel, gratuity
  • Client-facing payment portal (/pay)
  • Quote lifecycle: Draft → Sent → Accepted
  • Auto-sync accepted quotes to Carte calendar

Invoices, payments & reminders

When a quote is accepted, Yield auto-creates a deposit invoice and a balance invoice — numbered sequentially with your prefix. Record payments against them, and Yield automatically sends payment reminders at 7, 3, and 0 days before the due date. When the tab closes, you get a gold toast.

  • Auto-generated deposit & balance invoices
  • Sequential invoice numbers (INV-001 format)
  • PDF invoices with your logo & bank details
  • Automated payment reminders via email
  • 5-step tab timeline per job
