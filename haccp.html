Common questions about HACCP for private chefs.

Do private chefs need HACCP records in the UK?

Yes. Under UK food safety law, any food business operator — including private chefs working in clients' homes — must have a food safety management system based on HACCP principles. This includes keeping records of temperature checks, allergen management, cleaning and deliveries. The Food Standards Agency (FSA) provides guidance for small food businesses, and private chefs fall within scope. See our full guide: Do private chefs need HACCP in the UK?

Can I use a digital app instead of paper HACCP records?

Yes. Environmental Health Officers accept digital records. What matters is that your records are complete, timestamped and accessible. Veriqo produces EHO-ready PDF exports in one tap — more complete and more reliable than paper logs, which can be lost, damaged or incomplete.

What temperature thresholds does Veriqo use?

Veriqo uses UK FSA temperature guidelines as its defaults: fridges should be 0–5°C (warn above 5°C, fail above 8°C), freezers below −18°C, cooked food must reach at least 75°C, transport of cold food must stay below 5°C. These thresholds are built in and flag automatically when readings are out of range.

Does Veriqo work without internet access?

Yes. Veriqo is a Progressive Web App that stores records locally on your device first, then syncs to the cloud when you have a connection. You can log checks in a client's kitchen with no Wi-Fi and know your records are safe.

How is Veriqo different from other HACCP apps?

Most HACCP apps are built for restaurants with fixed kitchens, static fridge setups, and full-time teams. Veriqo is built specifically for private chefs — it has transport temperature logs, a private chef job mode, mobile kitchen credentials, and it integrates with the Menus and Costing modules so your food safety records are connected to your actual jobs.
