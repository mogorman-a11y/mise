Built to support your 5-star food hygiene rating

The Food Standards Agency rates every UK food business on a 0–5 scale under the Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (FHRS). A top rating isn't just about being clean on the day — an environmental health officer will also assess your confidence in food safety management, and that confidence comes from documented records.

Veriqo gives you a complete, date-stamped paper trail. Every temperature check, cleaning log, and allergen declaration is stored against your account and exportable on demand. Whether you're a private chef registering for the first time or an established caterer preparing for a scheduled inspection, Veriqo keeps you ready.

One app. Everything connected.

Every module shares the same data. Dishes you build in Menus feed directly into HACCP's allergen matrix and Costing's ingredient library. A quote accepted in Costing appears as a booking in Menus' calendar. Log a delivery in HACCP and Costing prompts you to log the supplier cost. Enter once — flow everywhere.

Veriqo is a progressive web app (PWA). Install it on your iPhone or Android home screen from getveriqo.co.uk/app and it works like a native app, even offline. Your records are encrypted, stored securely in the cloud, and backed up every time you save.

The HACCP module is designed to meet the record-keeping requirements of the UK Food Safety Act 1990 and the Food Standards Agency's Safer Food Better Business guidance for caterers. All logs are date-stamped, stored with your account, and can be exported as a plain-text report.
