HACCP Temperature Log — Free Printable Template

A ready-to-use temperature log covering fridge checks, cooking records, cooling logs and delivery checks — the core records an EHO expects to see from a private chef.

In your browser's print dialog, choose A4 landscape and disable headers & footers for the cleanest result.

How to use this log

  • Date — the date of the check, not the job date if different
  • Meal / Service — which service or event this record relates to
  • Food Item — what was checked (e.g. "Chicken breast", "Fridge A", "Beef delivery")
  • Check Type — use the key below the table: F = Fridge, Fz = Freezer, C = Cook, R = Reheat, Cl = Cool, D = Delivery
  • Temp (°C) — the actual probe or thermometer reading
  • Pass / Fail — tick or circle against the temperature guide at the bottom
  • Corrective Action — what you did if the reading was outside limits (e.g. "discarded", "rechecked after 10 min")
  • Initials — who took the reading
