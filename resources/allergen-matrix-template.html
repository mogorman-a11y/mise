The 14 regulated allergens under Natasha's Law

UK food law (the Food Information to Consumers Regulation) requires food businesses — including private chefs — to declare the presence of 14 major allergens in any food they provide. The allergens are:

Celery Cereals containing gluten Crustaceans Eggs Fish Lupin Milk Molluscs Mustard Tree nuts Peanuts Sesame Soya Sulphur dioxide / sulphites

For private chefs, this means knowing the allergen content of every dish you serve, disclosing it to clients before or at the point of service, and keeping documentation of what you disclosed and when.
