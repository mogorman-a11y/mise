From your first client enquiry to a completed event — Carte keeps every job, every menu, and every client detail exactly where you need it.

Client CRM & booking calendar

Keep a full history for every client — contact details, dietary requirements, past menus, and notes. Schedule bookings on a calendar view, manage unavailable dates, and never double-book again.

  • Client address book with full history
  • Monthly calendar with booking indicators
  • Unavailable date blocking
  • First inquiry script templates

Menu & dish library

Build a dish library with allergen information, then combine dishes into reusable menus. When you attach a menu to a booking, Carte freezes a snapshot of it — so a future change to your library never affects a past event.

  • Dish library with 14-allergen tagging
  • Saved menu templates
  • Frozen per-event menu snapshots
  • ✨ Magic Menu Importer (photo → dishes)

Shared data with Veriqo & Yield

Clients and menus you add in Carte flow automatically into Veriqo and Yield. A booking in Carte becomes a job in Yield's quote pipeline and an allergen matrix in Veriqo. Enter once — flow everywhere.

  • Allergen matrix auto-generated per event
  • Jobs appear in Yield for costing & invoicing
  • Freelancer access link for event-day handoff
  • Positioning one-pager PDF export
