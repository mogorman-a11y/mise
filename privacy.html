1. Who we are

Veriqo is business software for private chefs and caterers. It is operated by Side Order Catering Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales.

  • Trading name: Veriqo
  • Website: https://getveriqo.co.uk
  • Contact for privacy questions: hello@getveriqo.co.uk

For the personal information described in this policy where we act as controller, Side Order Catering Ltd is the controller.

We are confirming our registration position with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Where a registration reference is required and confirmed, it will be published here.

2. When this policy applies

This policy applies to personal information we handle as controller — that is, where we decide why and how it is processed. This mainly covers:

  • people who create or hold a Veriqo account;
  • people who contact us or use our website;
  • billing, security, service-operation and (optional) analytics data connected with an account.

Where a chef or business uses Veriqo to record information about their own clients, guests, staff or suppliers, that business decides why the information is collected and used. In that situation the business is the controller and we act as their processor. Section 5 and our Data Processing Terms explain how that works.

3. Our role as controller and processor

We wear two hats:

  • As a controller — for account, subscription, billing, communications, security and optional analytics information about the people who use Veriqo directly. This policy governs that processing.
  • As a processor — for the client, guest, staff, supplier and booking information that our customers enter into Veriqo to run their own businesses. We process that on the customer's documented instructions and only to provide the service. The customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for it, for telling their own data subjects how their information is used, and for handling requests from those people. Our processor commitments are set out in the Data Processing Terms.

4. Information we collect (as controller)

Account information

  • your name and your business name;
  • your email address;
  • your password, which is stored in hashed form by our authentication provider — we cannot see it;
  • optional two-factor authentication settings if you turn them on;
  • your business logo and business contact details, if you add them for use on documents you generate.

Subscription and billing information

  • your plan, subscription status and trial dates;
  • identifiers from our payment provider (for example a Stripe customer or subscription ID);
  • records of payments and invoices for our own accounting.

Card and bank details are entered into payment pages hosted by Stripe. We do not receive or store full card numbers.

Client-payment (Stripe Connect) information

If you choose to take card payments from your own clients through Veriqo, we hold the status of your connected Stripe account and references to payments made through it. The payment itself is processed by Stripe.

Communications

  • messages you send us and our replies;
  • delivery, open, click and bounce events for emails we send you, reported by our email provider;
  • your marketing preferences, including an unsubscribe flag and the date it was set.

Technical and security information

Our hosting, database and email providers generate limited log data when the service is used. This can include IP address, device and browser information, and timestamps. We use it to run the service securely, investigate problems and prevent abuse. We do not build advertising profiles from it.

Optional analytics information

If — and only if — you choose "Allow analytics" in the app, Google Analytics and PostHog collect usage information such as pages and features viewed, actions taken, approximate location derived from IP address, and a device or browser identifier. This is off by default. See section 14.

5. Information our customers enter about other people (we act as processor)

When you use Veriqo to run your business you may enter personal information about other people, including:

  • Clients and guests: names, contact details, addresses, dietary requirements and allergies, booking details, menu preferences and free-text notes — including information submitted through a client intake form you share.
  • Staff and food handlers: names, contact details, training and qualification records, and illness or injury records where you choose to record them.
  • Suppliers: business and contact details.
  • Financial records: quotes, invoices and payment records that name your clients.

For all of this, you are the controller and we act on your instructions to store and process it so you can use the service. You decide the purpose, you are responsible for identifying a lawful basis (and, where relevant, an Article 9 condition — see section 6), and you are responsible for giving your own clients, guests and staff the privacy information they are entitled to. Section 18 explains what happens to this information when an account is deleted.

6. Special-category information

Some information entered into Veriqo can be special-category personal data under UK GDPR Article 9. In particular:

  • allergies, intolerances and medically-driven dietary requirements can be information about health;
  • some dietary requirements (for example halal or kosher) can reveal religious beliefs;
  • staff illness or injury records can be information about health.

Where our customers put this kind of information into Veriqo, the customer is the controller. The customer is responsible for identifying an appropriate Article 9 condition for their own circumstances and for meeting any associated requirements. We do not assume a single condition applies to every customer, because it depends on how each business operates.

We process special-category information only as a processor, to provide the service on the customer's instructions. We do not use it for our own purposes, and our own controller processing (sections 4, 7 and 8) does not rely on special-category data.

7. How we use personal information (as controller)

  • to create and manage your account and let you sign in securely;
  • to provide the Veriqo service and the features on your plan;
  • to take subscription payments and keep proper billing and accounting records;
  • to enable client payments through Stripe Connect if you turn that on;
  • to send you service and transactional emails (for example sign-in links, password resets, trial and billing notices, and important service messages);
  • to provide support and respond to your messages;
  • to keep the service secure, maintain records, investigate problems and prevent fraud, abuse and misuse;
  • with your consent, to understand how the app is used through optional analytics;
  • where permitted by law, to send you product updates and marketing about Veriqo, which you can stop at any time;
  • to handle complaints, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
  • to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations;
  • in connection with a reorganisation, merger or sale of our business.

9. How AI features process information

Veriqo has optional AI-assisted features, such as importing a menu from a photo or text, scanning a receipt or label, drafting a short business bio, and generating cost estimates. These features only run when you choose to use them.

When you use one, the content you submit for that task (for example a menu photo, a receipt image, or the text you provide) is sent to OpenAI to generate the result, and the result is returned to Veriqo.

Based on OpenAI's published API data-usage terms, content submitted through the OpenAI API is not used to train OpenAI's models, and OpenAI retains API content only for a limited period for abuse monitoring before deleting it, unless it is required to keep it for legal reasons. We recommend not entering information into AI features that is not needed for the task.

To be confirmed: the exact OpenAI data-processing terms, retention period and transfer safeguards that apply to our account should be verified against OpenAI's current official documentation and recorded.

Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, we rely on UK "adequacy" regulations where they apply to the destination, or on appropriate safeguards such as the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional measures needed. We do not claim that a particular mechanism covers a particular provider unless we have confirmed it.

12. How long information is kept

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining appropriate business and financial records, resolving disputes, enforcing our agreements and meeting legal obligations. In practice:

  • Business records you enter (HACCP logs, bookings, clients, menus, costings, quotes, invoices) are kept while your account is active. We do not apply a separate Veriqo deletion schedule to them — they are yours to keep, edit or delete.
  • Payment-provider event records received from Stripe are deleted after 90 days.
  • Account deletion: when you complete the self-service deletion process, active-account data is designed to be deleted — including database records, your authentication account, and files you own in storage — and any active subscription is cancelled. This is subject to provider backups, information we must keep to meet legal obligations, and records we may legitimately need to retain (for example billing and accounting records, and records relating to a dispute).
  • Backups and provider logs: our providers keep backups and operational logs for limited periods according to their own retention cycles. We cannot guarantee immediate erasure from every backup system; backups are overwritten or expire in the normal course.

To be confirmed: exact backup retention windows and provider log-retention periods should be established with each provider and reflected here.

13. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • encryption of data in transit;
  • row-level security in the database so that one account cannot read another account's records;
  • authenticated access, with optional two-factor authentication;
  • access controls and use of reputable infrastructure providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential and for signing out on shared devices.

14. Browser storage and analytics

Our public marketing website currently uses no analytics or tracking technologies.

The signed-in Veriqo app uses necessary browser storage to work — for example your sign-in session, your app preferences, offline caches so the app works without a connection, and the record of your cookie choice itself. This storage does not need consent because the app cannot function without it.

Optional analytics — Google Analytics and PostHog — are switched off until you choose "Allow analytics". You can change your choice at any time through Cookie & analytics preferences in Settings. If you turn analytics off after having turned it on, we stop further analytics collection and remove the analytics identifiers we can. Analytics are never required to use the service. Full detail is on our Cookies & Similar Technologies page.

15. Marketing communications

  • Service and transactional emails — such as sign-in links, password resets, security notices and billing messages — are part of providing the service. You cannot opt out of these while you have an account.
  • Product and marketing emails about Veriqo may be sent where this is permitted by applicable law, including the PECR direct-marketing rules.
  • Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to opt out.
  • Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop service-critical emails.

To be confirmed: whether the PECR "soft opt-in" is available for existing customers depends on how their email address was obtained and whether an opt-out opportunity was given at that time. This should be reviewed before relying on it.

16. Your data-protection rights

Under UK data-protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is used (this policy);
  • request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete your information in certain circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict processing in certain circumstances;
  • ask for a copy of certain information in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting earlier processing;
  • complain to us, and to the ICO.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@getveriqo.co.uk. We may need to confirm your identity. We will respond within the time limits set by law. If you complain to us about how we have handled your personal information, we will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and keep you updated on the outcome.

If you are a client, guest or member of staff of a business that uses Veriqo, your request usually needs to go to that business as the controller — see section 17.

17. Account deletion and exports

You can delete your account and its data from Settings in the app, or by emailing us. Deletion is designed to remove your database records, your authentication account, and files you own in storage, and to cancel any active subscription. If the process cannot remove all of your stored files, it stops and asks you to contact us so we can complete it — it will not report success while sensitive files remain.

You can export your HACCP records as PDF or CSV, and your quotes and invoices as PDF, at any time while your account is active.

Some information may be retained after deletion as described in section 12.

18. If you are a Veriqo user's client or guest

If a chef or catering business has sent you an intake form or holds your details in Veriqo, that business decides how your information is used and is the controller for it. Veriqo provides the software they use to collect and store it.

Questions about your information, or requests to see, correct or delete it, should normally go to the chef or business you are dealing with. If you cannot reach them, you can contact us at hello@getveriqo.co.uk and we will help where we can, but we may need the business's instructions to act.
