Cookies & Similar Technologies

This page explains the cookies, browser storage and similar technologies Veriqo uses. UK rules on this ("PECR") are not limited to cookies — they also cover other ways of storing information on, or reading information from, your device, such as localStorage and IndexedDB. We cover all of it here.

Last updated 31 August 2026 · Related: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service

The public marketing website

Our public marketing website (pages like the home page, feature pages and resource articles) currently uses no analytics or tracking technologies. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Some pages load web fonts and, on payment-related pages only, technology from Stripe (see below).

The signed-in Veriqo app

The signed-in app (/app) needs to store some information in your browser to work at all. Separately, it can use optional analytics, but only if you turn them on.

Necessary browser storage

This is used to make Veriqo work. It does not require consent because the app cannot function without it, and it is not used to track you across other websites.

  • Sign-in session — keeps you signed in between page loads (stored by our authentication provider in localStorage).
  • App preferences — such as your theme, accent colour and the last area you were in.
  • Offline and local caches — copies of your recent records so the app keeps working without a connection.
  • Offline queues (IndexedDB) — changes made offline are queued in an IndexedDB database and sent when you reconnect.
  • Your cookie choice — the record of whether you have allowed analytics is itself stored so we can respect it.

Most of this local data is cleared when you sign out. Your preferences and your cookie choice are kept so the app behaves consistently next time.

Optional analytics — off until you choose "Allow analytics"

Google Analytics 4 and PostHog help us understand how the app is used. They do not load and set nothing until you choose "Allow analytics" in the consent banner. If you choose "Necessary only", neither is loaded.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Cookie & analytics preferences. If you turn analytics off after turning it on, we stop further collection and remove the analytics identifiers we can.

Payment technologies (Stripe)

When you or your client use a payment feature, or a Stripe-hosted page, Stripe may set cookies and use storage that are necessary for payment functionality, security and fraud prevention. These are used only in connection with payments.

Managing your choice

  • In Veriqo: open Settings → Cookie & analytics preferences to allow or turn off optional analytics at any time.
  • In your browser: you can block or delete cookies and clear site storage through your browser settings. Blocking necessary storage will stop the signed-in app from working properly.
