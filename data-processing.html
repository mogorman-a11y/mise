1. Scope and definitions

These Data Processing Terms ("DPT") apply to the extent that Side Order Catering Ltd ("Veriqo", "we") processes personal data on your behalf as your processor when you use the Veriqo service. You are the "Customer" and the controller of that personal data ("Customer Personal Data").

"UK GDPR", "controller", "processor", "personal data", "processing", "personal data breach", "special category data" and "data subject" have the meanings given in UK data-protection law, meaning the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003, each as amended (including by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025).

The Privacy Policy governs personal data for which Veriqo is itself the controller (for example your account and billing information). This DPT governs only Customer Personal Data.

3. Processing on your instructions

We will process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions, including in relation to international transfers, unless we are required to process it for other purposes by law — in which case we will inform you of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it.

Your instructions are made up of: these terms, the Terms of Service, the configuration and settings you choose in the service, and your use of the service's features. If you give us any additional instruction, we may charge a reasonable fee if acting on it is outside the scope of the service, or decline it if it is unlawful or not technically feasible.

4. Confidentiality

We ensure that people authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to an appropriate duty of confidentiality (whether contractual or statutory), and that access is limited to those who need it to provide the service or support.

5. Security measures

Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, as well as the risks to individuals, we implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data, including as appropriate:

  • encryption of data in transit;
  • logical separation of customer data, including row-level security so that one account cannot access another account's records;
  • authentication controls, including support for two-factor authentication;
  • role- and permission-based access within a customer's account or venue;
  • use of reputable infrastructure providers with their own security programmes;
  • measures to help restore availability and access to data in a timely manner in the event of an incident; and
  • processes for testing and reviewing the effectiveness of these measures.

We may update our security measures over time, provided the level of protection is not materially reduced.

7. International transfers

We and our sub-processors may process Customer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom, including in the European Economic Area and other countries. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we use or require an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism, which may include UK adequacy regulations, the UK's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as applicable, together with any additional measures needed. You instruct and authorise us to make such transfers for the purpose of providing the service.

8. Assisting with data-subject requests

The service provides features that let you access, correct, export and delete Customer Personal Data yourself. Taking into account the nature of the processing, we will also provide reasonable assistance by appropriate technical and organisational measures, so far as possible, to help you respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under UK data-protection law.

If we receive a request directly from one of your data subjects, we will not respond to it ourselves except on your instructions or as legally required, and we will promptly pass the request on to you where we can identify the relevant account.

9. Assisting with security, breaches and assessments

Taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to us, we will provide reasonable assistance to help you meet your obligations to:

  • keep Customer Personal Data secure;
  • notify personal data breaches to the ICO and to affected individuals where required; and
  • carry out data protection impact assessments and any prior consultation with the ICO.

We will notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and provide the information reasonably available to us to help you meet your notification obligations.

10. Return and deletion at the end

While your account is active, you can export Customer Personal Data using the service's export features (for example HACCP records as PDF or CSV, and quotes and invoices as PDF).

On termination or expiry of your account, or on your written request, we will delete Customer Personal Data, including by running the account-deletion process, except to the extent that we are required by law to retain it or legitimately need to retain limited records (for example billing records or records relating to a dispute). Copies present in routine backups will be deleted or overwritten in the ordinary course of our providers' backup cycles.

11. Information and audit

We will make available to you the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the UK GDPR and this DPT, and will allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by you or an auditor you appoint.

Given the nature of the service (a low-cost, standardised, multi-tenant SaaS product), audits will normally be satisfied by us providing responses to a reasonable security questionnaire and any relevant third-party certifications or reports we hold or can obtain from our sub-processors. An on-site or hands-on audit will be considered only where required by the ICO or by law, or where there are reasonable grounds to suspect a material breach, will be limited to once in any 12-month period unless a breach is being investigated, must be at your cost, must not disrupt the service or compromise the security or confidentiality of other customers' data, and is subject to reasonable advance notice and confidentiality undertakings.

12. Your obligations as controller

You confirm and agree that:

  • you have a lawful basis, and any additional condition required (for example for special category data), for the Customer Personal Data you process using Veriqo;
  • you have provided the privacy information your data subjects are entitled to, and obtained any consents required;
  • your instructions to us, and your use of the service, comply with UK data-protection law;
  • you will not use the service to process personal data that is excessive or irrelevant to your purpose; and
  • you are responsible for the accuracy, quality and legality of the Customer Personal Data and for how you use the outputs of the service.

13. Special-category data

You may use Veriqo to record allergy, dietary, health-related or belief-related information about your clients, guests or staff. Some of this is special category data. Deciding whether to record it, identifying an Article 9 condition, and meeting any associated requirements are your responsibility as controller. We process such data only to provide the service on your instructions and apply the security measures in section 5 to it.

14. Unlawful instructions

We will inform you if, in our opinion, an instruction from you infringes UK data-protection law, but we are not obliged to carry out a general legal review of your instructions and giving (or not giving) such notice does not reduce your responsibility as controller.
