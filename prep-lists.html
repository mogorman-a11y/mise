01

From menu to prep list
in one tap.

Auto-generated from your dishes

Add prep tasks to each dish in your Menus library — once. Every time you create a prep list for a job, Veriqo pulls those tasks automatically for whichever dishes are on the menu. No typing. No copy-pasting from last time.

Prep-ahead vs finishing

Every task is assigned to either prep-ahead (the day before) or finishing (on the day). Your prep list is split into these two sections so you can work through them in the right order without having to think about what happens when.

Grouped by course

Tasks within each section are grouped by course — Starter, Fish Course, Main, Dessert and so on — following a logical kitchen order. Switch between Prep-ahead and Finishing with one tap at the top of the list.

Tick off in the kitchen

Tap a task to tick it. Completed tasks move to the bottom of their section with a strikethrough so you always see what's left at a glance. Veriqo is a PWA, so the app opens even with a patchy signal — ticks sync to Supabase as you go.

Editable on the fly

Need to tweak a task once the list is generated? Tap Edit on any item to change its description. Changes sync to Supabase so your list is consistent across your phone, iPad and any device you're using.

Reuse for repeat jobs

Running the same menu again? Reset all ticks in one tap and the list is ready to reuse. No need to regenerate. Prep lists are saved against the job, so you can also review what you did last time — useful for regular private chef clients.

02

Build once,
use every time.

Step 01
Add prep tasks to your dishes

In the Menus module, open any dish and add its prep tasks — describe each step and assign it to prep-ahead or finishing. Most chefs do this once for their core repertoire and it pays back on every single job afterwards.

Step 02
Generate from a job

When you've confirmed a booking and built the menu, go to Prep Lists and tap Generate. Veriqo pulls prep tasks for every dish on the menu and organises them by section and course. Done in under 10 seconds.

Step 03
Work through it in the kitchen

Open the prep list on your phone on the day before (prep-ahead section) and on the day (finishing section). Tick tasks as you complete them. Everything syncs across devices, so your commis can work from the same list.

03

Common questions about Veriqo Prep Lists.

Do I need to write a prep list manually for every job?

No. Add prep tasks to each dish in your Menus library once, and Veriqo pulls the relevant tasks automatically every time you generate a prep list for a job — no retyping, no copy-pasting from a previous event.

What's the difference between "prep-ahead" and "finishing" tasks?

Prep-ahead tasks are steps you'd typically do the day before an event — marinating, par-cooking, prepping garnishes. Finishing tasks are what happens on the day itself. Veriqo splits your prep list into these two sections automatically so you can work through the right tasks at the right time.

Can I use Veriqo Prep Lists without a phone signal?

Yes. Veriqo is a Progressive Web App that stores your prep list locally on your device, so it loads and stays usable even without Wi-Fi or signal in a client's kitchen — everything syncs once you're back online.

Is Prep Lists available on its own, or only with the rest of Veriqo?

Prep Lists is part of the Veriqo Pro plan, alongside HACCP, Menus & booking management, and Costing — all sharing the same dish library so nothing needs entering twice. See pricing on the homepage.
