01

Everything from booking to the kitchen door.

Job & booking management

Log each job with client name, event date, number of covers, location and notes. See all your upcoming bookings at a glance. No more hunting through emails to find out what you agreed with a client.

Menu builder

Build your dish library once — name, course, description, allergens and prep tasks. Then create menus for each job by selecting dishes. Menus carry allergen information through automatically, so you're never guessing.

Client dietary tracking

Record dietary requirements and allergen information per client. When you create a menu for a job, you'll always have a clear picture of what can and can't be served — and a documented record that you had it.

Auto-generated prep lists

Once you've added prep tasks to your dishes, Veriqo can generate a full prep list for any job in one tap — split by prep-ahead and finishing, organised by course. See Prep Lists for more.

Job notes & transport log

Add notes to each job — parking, kitchen layout, client preferences, access codes. Log transport temperatures from the job record, keeping your HACCP and job records in sync without double-entering anything.

Connected to Costing

Dishes in your Menus library are the same dishes you cost in the Costing module. Price a job, generate a quote and issue an invoice — all without re-entering information you've already saved. See Costing.

Connected to HACCP

The food library you build in Menus is shared with the HACCP module. Dishes you add here appear automatically in your cooking and allergen logs — so you never have to enter a dish name twice.

02

From enquiry to service, organised.

Step 01
Build your dish library

Add the dishes you regularly cook — name, course, description, allergens and prep tasks. This is the foundation of your menus and prep lists. Once it's built, creating a menu for a new job takes minutes.

Step 02
Log the booking

When a client confirms, create a job in Veriqo — client, date, covers, location, dietary requirements. Select the dishes for the menu from your library. A prep list generates automatically from the job.

Step 03
Go to service

On the day, open Veriqo to see your prep list, check off tasks as you complete them, and log HACCP checks from the same app. After service, generate the invoice from the Costing module — connected to the same job.

03

Common questions about Veriqo Menus.

Is Veriqo Menus available on its own, or only as part of Veriqo Pro?

Menus & booking management is part of the Veriqo Pro plan, alongside HACCP, Costing and Prep Lists — all connected and sharing the same dish library and job records. See pricing on the homepage.

How does Veriqo track allergens and dietary requirements per client?

You record dietary requirements against each client once. When you build a menu for a job, Veriqo shows you what can and can't be served against those requirements, and keeps a documented record that you had that information — useful if a client ever asks what was checked.

Does building a menu in Veriqo also generate my prep list?

Yes. Once you've added prep tasks to the dishes in your library, Veriqo can auto-generate a full prep list for any job in one tap — split into prep-ahead and finishing tasks, grouped by course. See Prep Lists for more.

Can I track bookings without a fixed venue — cooking in different clients' homes?

Yes. Each job in Veriqo records the client, date, covers and location independently, so it works whether you cook from one kitchen or travel to a different client's home for every booking.
